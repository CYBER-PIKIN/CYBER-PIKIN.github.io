TryHackMe & Hack The Box Learning Labs

Guided and independent practice to build a methodical approach to security assessment and problem-solving.

Learning goals

  • Practice scoping and enumerating lab targets.
  • Build familiarity with Linux, networking, and common security tools.
  • Develop a hypothesis-driven workflow: observe, test, validate, and document.
  • Improve technical note-taking and the ability to explain what a finding means.

Lab workflow

  1. Read the room or machine instructions and stay within its authorized scope.
  2. Enumerate carefully and keep notes of commands and observations.
  3. Validate findings rather than relying on assumptions.
  4. Document lessons learned and defensive implications.
